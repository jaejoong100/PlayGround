How to play

Two players share one keyboard. Each round deals a random quick-fire challenge. Follow the cue and be faster (or more accurate) than your opponent.

  • Player 1: press A or Space
  • Player 2: press L or Enter
  • On phones, tap your own colored button.
  • In Memory Flip, Player 1 uses A / D and Player 2 uses J / L.
  • False starts cost a point. First to 5 points takes the crown!
