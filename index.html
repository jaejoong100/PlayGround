How to play

Two players share one screen. Each round deals a random challenge. The stage is split in half — tap anywhere on your side or use your keys. Both players can tap at the exact same time.

  • Player 1: left half, A or Space
  • Player 2: right half, L or Enter
  • Memory Flip: P1 uses A / D, P2 uses J / L
  • Odd One Out and Number Rush are tap-the-tiles rounds.
  • False starts and wrong buttons give the point away. First to 5 points wins!
